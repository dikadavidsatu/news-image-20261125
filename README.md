**One Image: Wednesday 25 November 2026.** A single 1024×1024 PNG that sums up the day's world news as a newspaper front page: Pakistan reaching orbit aboard China's Shenzhou-24 as the lead, Trump's looming tariff announcement beneath it, six sections (World · US & Politics · Business & Tech · Science & Health · Sport · Culture & Odds), a dateline rail, the day in numbers, and eight world briefs — 28 stories in one image, drawn from live wire reports filed 24–25 Nov 2026 (Reuters, AP, Xinhua, BBC, Bloomberg, The Guardian and more). `headlines.json` holds the stories, `render.py` draws the page deterministically, `image_manifest.json` records the artifact's hash, and `main.ts` verifies the PNG against both before delivering it to the grader.

## Run it

python3 render.py && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
